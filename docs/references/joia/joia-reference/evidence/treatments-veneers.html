Porcelain Veneers.

Hand-layered porcelain, shaped and shaded to match your natural enamel. Built to sit with your face, not stand out from it.

What it is

Thin porcelain, designed around your face.

A veneer is a thin layer of porcelain bonded to the front of a tooth. Done well, it corrects shape, shade, and proportion without looking like a set of teeth.

We make ours by hand in our own lab, matched to your enamel and the lines of your face, so the result reads as yours.

How it goes

From first visit to finished smile.

01

Good to know

Common questions.

Do veneers look fake?

Not when they're designed around your face and made by hand. The goal is teeth that look like they were always yours.

How long do they last?

With good care, porcelain veneers commonly last ten to fifteen years or more.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel little to nothing. We use local anaesthetic where needed and keep tooth preparation minimal.

How many visits does it take?

Usually two to three: consultation and design, preparation, then the fit.
